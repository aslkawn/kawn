# Kawn roadmap

## MVP (4-6 weeks solo)
Map (Dubai/AD/Sharjah switcher) + flights (DXB/DWC/AUH) + metro/bus + real estate layer; AR/EN RTL; dark/light; share link.
## v1
Weather/dust, traffic, vessels, City brief card, City stories (timeline of growth), persona modes, voice (EN then AR).
## v2
Cockpit-style flight ride, annotations by voice, satellite imagery before/after, MCP tools, embed.
## Open decisions
- Domain: kawn.dev ($17) or kawnly.com ($16); not bought. Trademark search not done.
- Stack: MapLibre GL + deck.gl, TypeScript, small Node proxy.
- Data licences: verify DLD open data, RTA GTFS, OpenSky/ADS-B terms for commercial use.
- Voice: local (speech-to-speech) vs hosted; Arabic quality test needed.
- Spec gaps to fill: weather sources, director, annotations, tools (use GitNexus/REA per 07).
